Our Story

Crafted with Pure Passion & Real Cocoa

At Chocobar, we believe great chocolate begins with patience, honest ingredients, and respect for traditional chocolatiers' craft. Every bar, truffle, and praline is handmade in small batches using 100% pure cocoa butter—never artificial substitutes or palm oils.

From rich single-origin dark blends to our signature Irish-inspired cream infusions, each creation is tempered by hand to achieve a glossy finish, crisp snap, and unforgettable melt.
