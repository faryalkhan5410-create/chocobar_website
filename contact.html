Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer custom gift wrapping?

Yes! Every gift box can be finished with a satin ribbon and a personalized message card at checkout.

Are your chocolates nut-free?

While we offer pure dark and milk varieties, all chocolates are crafted in a kitchen that handles hazelnuts, almonds, and pistachios.

How should I store my chocolates?

Store in a cool, dry place between 15°C and 18°C away from direct sunlight—avoid refrigerating to preserve the glossy snap.
